# Privacy Policy for FrostShift: Quantum Tap
Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the "FrostShift: Quantum Tap" mobile application. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

# Information Collection and Use
FrostShift: Quantum Tap is designed as an offline/local arcade game. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, phone number, device identifiers, or precise location.

# Game Data & Local Storage
All gameplay data, including high scores, combo statistics, sound preferences, and game settings, are stored locally on your device. This data is not transferred to, shared with, or stored on external servers.

# Third-Party Services
The application does not currently integrate or use third-party services, SDKs, or analytics tools that collect information used to identify you.

# Children's Privacy
Our application does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

# Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this document periodically for any updates or changes.
